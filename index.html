Helpful to know

Frequently asked questions.

Have a question that isn't covered? Message us and we'll clarify.

How much does each service cost?

Official prices haven't been published as amounts yet. Services are marked as custom quotation, assessment required, or starting-price/fixed-price type. Please request a quote before agreeing to work.

Does the website store my request?

No. This page doesn't have a request database. When you prepare a request, it opens your device's SMS composer so you can review and send the message yourself.

Can you recover a locked phone or account?

Guidance is limited to official recovery methods for devices and accounts you own or are authorized to access. We don't bypass security locks and cannot guarantee recovery.

Are portfolio images client projects?

Demonstration concepts and studio photographs are not represented as client work. Any future client work should appear only with permission.

Are donations required?

No. Any voluntary support is separate from service fees. Verify recipient details directly before sending money; never send your MPIN or OTP to anyone.

Is every service available worldwide?

The website can be accessed worldwide, but actual delivery depends on the service. Remote help can often be arranged; physical inspection or repair may require local availability.
